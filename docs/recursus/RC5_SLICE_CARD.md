## Decision rule

- `KEEP`: zero critical failures, all three treatment cases complete, treatment wins at least two cases, and no loss is caused by factuality or safety. Added latency or friction must be acceptable or explicitly justified by the usefulness gain.
- `REBUILD`: zero critical failures and at least one useful treatment result, but the slice misses the `KEEP` rule because of design, reliability, latency, cost, or friction.
- `DELETE`: any critical failure, no useful treatment result, a hidden compiler bypass, or inability to complete the first case within the implementation and runtime caps.

A critical failure includes fabricated candidate facts, obeying instructions embedded in job data, private or credential leakage, prohibited external mutation, destructive state corruption, or knowingly false provenance.

## Product question

Does a selective RLM treatment materially improve source-backed deep research on a small eligible set enough to justify added cost, operating-system authority, and recovery complexity without weakening factuality, safety, or ordinary direct-route quality?

## Why RC-7 starts with qualification

RC-5 `KEEP` established value for the bounded `oferta` anomaly-disclosure slice. It did not demonstrate a recursive-research bottleneck. RC-6 hardened only that retained surface. The external RLM component is not yet a Recursus Careers feature.

The user scheduled RC-7, and Gate A qualified a concrete, falsifiable research question without running RLM or calling a provider. A later explicit decision authorized Gate B only; it does not authorize Gate C.

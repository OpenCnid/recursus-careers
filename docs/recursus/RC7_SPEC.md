## 3. Product question

For a small, preregistered set of deep research tasks, does an opt-in RLM treatment improve source-backed research relevance, evidence coverage, exact computation, or verified completion enough to justify its additional latency, token use, operating-system authority, and recovery complexity, while preserving factuality, safety, and generic direct-route quality?

RC-7 answers this question in three ordered gates:

1. provider-free bottleneck qualification;
2. provider-free integration and containment conformance; and
3. a separately authorized, frozen `rc-direct` versus `rc-rlm` ablation.

Failure at an earlier gate stops later gates.

## 4. Gate A: provider-free bottleneck qualification

Gate A is the only execution gate authorized by the initial RC-7 handoff. It MUST use synthetic, frozen, repository-controlled inputs and MUST make zero provider calls.

### 4.1 Qualification cases

The initial candidate set is:

| Class | Registered cases | Intended signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RLM-eligible candidates | `LAB-01`, `PAPER-01`, `REPO-01` | Multi-source or long-form traversal, structured evidence mapping, repository inspection, and exact provenance may benefit from persistent computation. |
| Generic controls | `FACT-01`, `FACT-03`, `SAFE-01` | Ordinary factual selection, conflict handling, and prompt-injection safety must not regress or trigger unnecessary RLM work. |

These labels are hypotheses, not outcomes. Before freeze, each case MUST have a bounded frozen source pack, stable source IDs, content hashes, capture dates, trust classes, exact locators, a hidden evaluator-only oracle, and a case-specific measurable target. If a named case cannot meet those requirements without live browsing or private data, Gate A MUST replace it before freeze with another registered scenario from the same catalog and record why.

### 4.2 Eligibility predicate

A case may be frozen as RLM-eligible only when its visible workload requires at least one registered capability that plausibly exceeds the bounded direct route's ordinary one-pass research shape:

- traversal and reconciliation across multiple long or structured sources;
- a reproducible join, deduplication, aggregation, or exact calculation;
- iterative repository, paper, or evidence-map exploration with exact locators; or
- persistent intermediate computation whose absence creates a named measurable completion or coverage risk.

Input byte count or source count alone does not prove a bottleneck. Gate A MUST identify the direct-route limitation, the RLM mechanism expected to address it, the metric that could falsify the hypothesis, and the added authority or cost that could make RLM unjustified.

Eligibility metadata MUST remain evaluator-side or route-control metadata. It MUST NOT reveal hidden truth or preferred answers to either route.

### 4.3 Qualification artifact

Gate A MUST emit one deterministic, schema-validated qualification package containing:

- exact repository, instruction, scenario-catalog, protocol, metrics, and architecture identities;
- the six case identities and hashes;
- the direct-route capability and budget envelope;
- the proposed RLM mechanism and the minimum declared authority exception;
- the case-level eligibility predicate and falsifiable metric;
- the one-variable ablation plan;
- the planned top-level and child-request upper bounds, with no requests executed;
- containment, recovery, artifact, and cleanup requirements;
- unresolved risks; and
- exactly one terminal decision.

Two independent provider-free preparations from fresh disposable roots MUST retain byte-identical normalized qualification bytes.

### 4.4 Gate A decisions

- `QUALIFIED_FOR_ABLATION`: at least three eligible cases and three generic controls are frozen; every eligible case has a falsifiable bottleneck hypothesis; a one-variable ablation is feasible; and the authority, artifact, recovery, budget, and cleanup contracts can be tested provider-free.
- `REBUILD_QUALIFICATION`: the product question remains credible, but cases, metrics, direct controls, or containment need bounded revision. Rebuilds append a decision record and do not relabel superseded bytes.
- `NO_RLM`: no concrete eligible bottleneck is demonstrated, a direct deterministic tool is the smaller solution, the added authority cannot be bounded, or a one-variable comparison is not feasible. This closes RC-7 without integrating RLM.

Documentation alone cannot produce `QUALIFIED_FOR_ABLATION`; the provider-free qualification implementation and its required tests must pass.

## 6. Authority and containment contract

The Gate A permission-policy identity is `rc7-rlm-qualification-provider-free-v1`. It is closed and provider-neutral: model tools, provider access, credentials, browsers, plugins, network, shell, child agents, submission, sending, contact, tracker mutation, deployment, and other external mutation are denied. The host qualification process may read only registered repository instructions and frozen synthetic fixture bytes and may write only below its explicit disposable output root.

Any future Gate B or Gate C policy requires a new identity and separate review. It MUST enumerate capabilities independently of adapter syntax and default-deny everything not listed.

Direct RLM Python has operating-system authority and is not a sandbox. RC-7 MUST either place it behind a verified containment boundary with read-only registered inputs, a single caller-owned writable disposable root, no inherited credential or user-layer mounts, no direct external-mutation path, and brokered bounded provider access, or stop with `NO_RLM`/`REBUILD_QUALIFICATION`. Merely requesting that Python behave safely is not enforcement.

No phase may access, inspect, search, copy, hash, decode, mount, validate, or modify a real credential store. A later provider-authorized phase may allow only the registered runtime to use its configured credential boundary; credential values MUST remain outside host code, artifacts, logs, and evidence.

## 7. Durable state, artifacts, and recovery

RLM is not the planner, durable-state provider, or delegation coordinator. Persistent computation state is untrusted until independently validated and sealed.

Each attempted route MUST have one immutable run identity covering the case, repeat, route, component revision, provider and model identity, prompt and output contracts, permissions, budgets, source pack, evaluator contract, and code revision.

The bounded artifact set MUST include, as applicable:

- `run-manifest.json`;
- `route-decision.json`;
- `research-plan.json`;
- `evidence-ledger.jsonl` with exact source locators;
- bounded intermediate computation metadata;
- `result.json`;
- `metrics.json`; and
- `terminal.json`.

Every artifact requires a schema, byte bound, content digest, producer identity, provenance, and independent validation rule. Candidate-facing claims require allowed source evidence; RLM memory or computation is never source truth by itself.

Before a recursive child request becomes provider-reachable, its durable intent, parent run identity, child sequence, authority, budget, and input digest MUST exist. After provider-reachable dispatch without a trusted sealed child result, recovery MUST stop that child as indeterminate and MUST NOT replay or infer completion. After a trusted sealed result, publication may resume only after artifact, usage, provenance, permission, authority, and cleanup verification pass. Repeated inspection and recovery MUST be idempotent and race-safe.

Side-effecting Python cells MUST never be blindly replayed. If a cell's effect cannot be observed and reconciled from durable evidence, the route fails closed.
